# Flag Day Explorer

A UAE Flag Day QR hunt for pupils aged 6 to 9 at Repton Abu Dhabi, in English and Arabic.

Pupils scan the QR posters around the school with a school iPad. Each poster opens its own game; after all 15 games the flag reaches the top of the pole. Names and progress stay on the iPad, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Website: https://waedfoudeh.github.io
